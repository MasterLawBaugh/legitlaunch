Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Legit Launch (“Legit Launch,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides business launch, intellectual property research, entity formation, compliance, and reporting tools for entrepreneurs and businesses.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be processed by service providers, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Legit Launch, we may collect or process:

  • Account information: Information such as your email address, Firebase user identifier, authentication status, and email-verification status. Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. Legit Launch does not directly store your plaintext password.
  • Business and project information: Information you provide while using Legit Launch, such as business or brand names, goods and services descriptions, business activities, industry classifications, NAICS information, formation state, entity type, and other project information.
  • Research and report information: Search requests, trademark research, business-registry research, market-use research, formation information, saved project data, generated reports, and related results used to provide Legit Launch features.
  • Purchase and subscription information: Information needed to determine whether you purchased a Launch Report or have an active Founder Pro subscription, including product, transaction, subscription, entitlement, and account-linked purchase information. Payments are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Legit Launch does not receive or store your complete payment-card information.
  • App and service information: Technical or operational information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Legit Launch.
  • Support communications: Information contained in messages you send to us for customer support or other assistance.

How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • Verify email addresses and support password-reset requests.
  • Save and restore business projects and research.
  • Provide Idea Validation, IP Protection, Entity Formation, and related tools.
  • Generate, store, and deliver Integrated Launch Reports and related reports.
  • Determine subscription, purchase, report-credit, and feature-access status.
  • Provide compliance and business-launch information.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and functionality of the service.

Service Providers

Legit Launch uses third-party service providers to operate portions of the service. These may include:

  • Google Firebase: Used for authentication, email verification, password reset, application data storage, and backend application functionality.
  • Apple StoreKit: Used to process in-app purchases and subscriptions and to provide verified transaction and entitlement information.
  • Research, infrastructure, and technology providers: Certain research, search, report-generation, email-delivery, or AI-assisted functionality may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide requested Legit Launch features. Information provided to these services is limited to what is reasonably necessary to perform the requested function.

These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices and only as necessary to provide their respective services.

Payments

Consumer purchases of Legit Launch digital products and subscriptions are processed through Apple’s App Store.

Legit Launch may receive transaction identifiers, product identifiers, subscription status, and related entitlement information necessary to verify access to purchased features. Legit Launch does not directly collect or store your full payment-card information.

Sale of Personal Information and Advertising

Legit Launch does not sell your personal information. Legit Launch does not use your personal information for third-party behavioral advertising.

Data Sharing

We may disclose information:

  • To service providers necessary to operate Legit Launch and provide requested functionality.
  • When required by law, court order, subpoena, or other valid legal process.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, property, or integrity of Legit Launch, its users, or others.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of some or all of the business, subject to applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain account, project, transaction, research, and report information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Legit Launch, maintain legitimate business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, and protect the service.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is maintained.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction, or deletion.

Legit Launch also allows users to permanently delete their account directly from within the app.

For privacy or account-data questions, contact: support@legitlaunch.app

Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your Legit Launch account from the Profile section of the app.

When account deletion is successfully completed, Legit Launch deletes the associated Firebase Authentication account and user-owned application data covered by the deletion process.

Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate transactional, fraud-prevention, security, accounting, or operational purposes. Apple may also independently retain App Store transaction history under its own policies.

Deleting your Legit Launch account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store subscription. App Store subscriptions must be managed through Apple.

Email Verification and Password Reset

Legit Launch uses Firebase Authentication to verify email addresses and provide password-reset functionality.

Verification and password-reset emails may be sent to the email address associated with your account.

Children’s Privacy

Legit Launch is intended for business owners, entrepreneurs, and other adult users and is not directed to children under 13 or the applicable minimum age required by law.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed through Legit Launch. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Legal and Professional Information

Legit Launch provides business research, organizational tools, and informational guidance. Information presented through the service is not a substitute for advice from a licensed attorney, accountant, tax professional, or other qualified professional when professional advice is appropriate.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Legit Launch evolves. When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date shown above.
