Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Legit Launch (“Legit Launch,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides business launch, intellectual property research, entity formation, compliance, and reporting tools for entrepreneurs and businesses.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be processed by service providers, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Legit Launch, we may collect or process:

  • Account information: Information such as your email address and authentication credentials used to create and access your account. Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. Legit Launch does not directly store your plaintext password.
  • Business and project information: Information you provide while using Legit Launch, such as business or brand names, goods and services descriptions, business activities, industry classifications, NAICS information, formation state, entity type, and other project information.
  • Research and report information: Search requests, trademark research, business-registry research, market-use research, formation information, saved project data, generated reports, and related results used to provide Legit Launch features.
  • Purchase and subscription information: Information needed to determine whether you purchased a Launch Report or have an active Founder Pro subscription. Payments are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Legit Launch does not receive your complete payment-card information from Apple.
  • App and service information: Technical or operational information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Legit Launch.
  • Support communications: Information contained in messages you send to us for customer support or other assistance.

How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • Save and restore business projects and research.
  • Provide Idea Validation, IP Protection, Entity Formation, and related tools.
  • Generate, store, and deliver Launch Reports.
  • Determine subscription, purchase, and feature-access status.
  • Provide compliance and business-launch information.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and functionality of the service.

Service Providers

Legit Launch uses third-party service providers to operate portions of the service. These may include:

  • Google Firebase: Used for services including authentication, application data storage, and backend application functionality.
  • Apple StoreKit: Used to process in-app purchases and subscriptions and to provide verified transaction and entitlement information.

These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices and only as necessary to provide their respective services.

Sale of Personal Information and Advertising

Legit Launch does not sell your personal information to advertisers. Legit Launch does not use your personal information for third-party behavioral advertising.

Data Retention

We retain account, project, transaction, research, and report information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Legit Launch, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is maintained.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction, or deletion.
